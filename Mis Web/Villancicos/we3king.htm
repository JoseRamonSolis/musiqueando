We Three Kings

 

1.-We three Kings of Orient are;  Bearing gifts we traverse afar;

Field And fountain, moor and mountain, Following yonder star;

 

CHORUS:

O star of wonder, star of night, star with royal beauty bright,

Westward leading still proceeding, Guide us to thy perfect light.

 

2.-MELCHIOR: Born a King on Bethlehem plain, Gold I bring, to crown him again –

King for ever, ceasing never, Over us all to reign:

CHORUS

3.-CASPAR: Frankincense to offer have I; Incense owns a Deity night:

Prayer and praising, all men raising, Worship him, God most high:

CHORUS

4.-BALTHAZAR: Myrrh is mine; its bitter perfume Breathes a life of gathering gloom

Sorrowing, singhing, bleeding, dying, Sealed in the stone-cold tomb:

CHORUS

5.-Glorious now, behold him arise; King, and God, and sacrifice,

Heaven sing alleluya, Alleluya the earth replies:

CHORUS
